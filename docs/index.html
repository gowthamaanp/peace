Features

Small, daily habits. A clearer picture over time.

Everything in Peace is built around one idea: check in regularly, notice patterns early, and know where to turn for help.

😊

Log your mood in a few seconds

Each day, Peace asks one simple question: how do you feel today? Pick from Sad, Fine, Well or Excellent, and your answer is added to a 7-day tracker.

  • A 7-day view makes changes in mood easy to spot
  • A daily streak helps turn checking in into a habit
  • When you log a low mood, Peace gently offers to connect you with a counsellor
✨

A thought for the day, and a nudge to reflect

Every visit brings a short quote to reframe the day, followed by an easy path into a full self-assessment when you want to go deeper.

  • A fresh quote on each visit
  • The assessment is clearly timed (10–15 minutes), so you know what you're starting
🧠

Validated self-screening, one question at a time

Peace uses standardised questionnaires that healthcare professionals use around the world, split into short sections with a clear progress bar.

PHQ-9GAD-7PHQ-15PHQ panic moduleDASS-42
  • Covers depression, anxiety, stress, panic and physical symptoms
  • No time limit, with tips for answering honestly and calmly
  • DASS-42 is optional, taken after the PHQ sections
📈

See your progress, in plain language

Your latest scores appear on the home screen, with arrows showing how each one changed since your previous assessment. Every past result is saved in your history.

  • Scores for PHQ-9, GAD-7, PHQ-15 and each DASS-42 subscale
  • Each score comes with a plain-language explanation of its severity range
  • A dated test history you can open on your phone during a counselling session
📞

Help is always one tap away

"Talk with a Counselor" connects you to Tele-MANAS, the Government of India's free national tele-mental-health service. Tap Call Now and your phone dials it.

  • Toll-free: 14416 or 1-800 891 4416
  • Built into the home screen, not buried in a menu
🔒

Private, and in your control

You sign in with your phone number, so there's no password to remember. Your data is linked to your own account, and only that account can read it.

  • A built-in guide explains every questionnaire and its scoring
  • A clear privacy policy, one tap away in Settings
  • Light, dark or system theme
  • Delete your account at any time from Settings

For counsellors & institutions

A bridge between check-ins and conversations.

Peace was developed at Puducherry Technological University to make support easier to reach and to reduce stigma. Here's where it fits alongside a counselling service.

🧭

Earlier self-awareness

Daily mood logging and self-screening help people notice changes early, before they become harder to manage.

🗂️

Better-informed sessions

A student can open their dated history of standard scores on their phone and walk through it with a counsellor, instead of trying to describe weeks from memory.

🤝

A clear path to help

A low mood brings up a prompt to talk to someone, and the Tele-MANAS helpline is always one tap from the home screen.

What Peace is

  • A private, self-directed check-in and screening app
  • Built on published, widely used instruments with standard scoring
  • A way to keep a personal record to share at your own choice

What Peace is not

  • A diagnostic tool or a replacement for professional care
  • A crisis service. In an emergency, call 112 or Tele-MANAS 14416
  • A monitoring tool. There's no dashboard for institutions to view anyone's results
