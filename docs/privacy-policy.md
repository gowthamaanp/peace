# Privacy Policy for Peace

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2025  
**Last Updated:** September 30, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to Peace ("we," "our," or "us"). Peace is a mental health and wellness application designed to support your emotional well-being through mood tracking, mental health assessments, and personalized analytics. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with care and transparency.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Peace mobile application (the "App"). By using Peace, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you register and use Peace, we collect the following personal information:

- **Name**: Your full name for profile identification
- **Phone Number**: Required for authentication via OTP (One-Time Password)
- **Email Address**: For communication and account recovery purposes
- **Age**: To provide age-appropriate content and support
- **Gender**: For personalized user experience
- **Register Number** (Optional): For institutional identification if applicable
- **Department** (Optional): For institutional affiliation if applicable

### 2. Emergency Contact Information

For safety and support purposes, we collect:

- **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of your designated emergency contact
- **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Phone number of your emergency contact
- **Preferred Contact Method**: Your preferred method of communication

### 3. Mental Health Data

Peace collects sensitive mental health information to provide you with assessment results and personalized insights:

- **Mood Tracking Data**: Daily mood entries including emoji selections, mood labels, dates, and timestamps
- **Mood Streaks**: Consecutive days of mood logging
- **Mental Health Assessment Results**:
  - DASS-42 (Depression, Anxiety, and Stress Scales) scores and responses
  - PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire) scores and responses
  - PHQ-15 assessment scores and responses
  - GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder) scores and responses
- **Assessment History**: Timestamps and historical records of all completed assessments
- **Test Result Analysis**: Severity classifications and trend data

### 4. User-Generated Content

- **Problem Reports**: Issues or problems you report through the app
- **Feedback**: Any feedback you provide about the app

### 5. Technical Information

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **Firebase Authentication Data**: User ID (UID) assigned by Firebase
- **Network Status**: Internet connectivity status to ensure app functionality
- **App Usage Data**: Features you use and interactions within the app

### 6. Location Information

We do **NOT** collect precise location data or GPS coordinates. However, we request:

- **Network State Permission**: To check internet connectivity and ensure the app functions properly
- **Internet Access**: Required for authentication, data synchronization, and cloud storage

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 1. Service Delivery

- **Authentication**: Verify your identity through phone number OTP authentication
- **Profile Management**: Create and maintain your user profile
- **Mood Tracking**: Record and display your daily mood entries and streaks
- **Mental Health Assessments**: Provide standardized mental health screening tools (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PHQ-15, DASS-42)
- **Analytics and Insights**: Generate personalized charts, graphs, and trend analysis of your mental health data
- **Motivational Support**: Display relevant quotes and supportive content

### 2. Communication

- **Emergency Support**: Enable emergency contact functionality when needed
- **Appointment Coordination**: Facilitate counselor appointment bookings
- **Service Updates**: Notify you of important app updates or changes (if applicable)

### 3. App Improvement

- **Problem Resolution**: Address technical issues and bugs reported by users
- **Feature Enhancement**: Improve app functionality based on usage patterns
- **Quality Assurance**: Ensure the app operates correctly and efficiently

### 4. Legal Compliance

- Comply with applicable laws and regulations
- Respond to legal requests and prevent fraud or abuse

## Data Storage and Security

### Cloud Storage

- Your data is stored securely in **Google Cloud Firestore**, a Firebase service
- All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS)
- We use Firebase Authentication for secure user identity management

### Local Storage

- Some data may be cached locally on your device using **Shared Preferences** for offline access and improved performance
- Local data is protected by your device's security measures

### Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication via phone OTP
- **Access Control**: Strict access controls limit who can view or modify your data
- **Firebase Security Rules**: Database security rules prevent unauthorized access
- **Regular Updates**: We regularly update our security measures and infrastructure

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information or mental health data to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

1. **Firebase (Google)**:

   - Firebase Authentication (phone authentication)
   - Cloud Firestore (database storage)
   - Firebase Core (app infrastructure)
   - Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2. **Flutter Framework**: Open-source mobile app framework (no data collection by Flutter itself)

### Limited Disclosure

We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

- **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize us to share your information
- **Emergency Situations**: To your designated emergency contact if there is a threat to your safety or health
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or government request
- **Service Providers**: To trusted service providers who assist in operating the app (all bound by confidentiality agreements)
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified)

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Correction

- You can access and update your personal information through the Profile section of the app
- You can modify your name, email, phone number, age, gender, and emergency contact information at any time

### Data Deletion

- **Account Deletion**: You can delete your account and all associated data through the app's settings
- When you delete your account, we permanently remove:
  - Your user profile and registration data
  - All mood tracking entries and streaks
  - All mental health assessment results and history
  - Appointment records
  - Problem reports
- **Note**: Some data may be retained for a limited period for legal compliance or legitimate business purposes

### Data Export

- You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at info.ptupeace@gmail.com

### Theme Preferences

- You can switch between light and dark themes without affecting data collection

### Opt-Out

- If you choose not to provide required information (phone number, name), you will not be able to use the app's core features
- You can uninstall the app at any time to stop data collection

## Children's Privacy

Peace is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our systems. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: We retain your data for as long as your account remains active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Data may be retained for a reasonable period after account inactivity
- **Deleted Accounts**: Upon account deletion, we permanently delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law
- **Legal Requirements**: Some data may be retained longer if required by applicable laws or regulations

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including servers in regions where Firebase/Google Cloud services operate. By using Peace, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make changes:

- We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Significant changes will be communicated through the app or via email
- Your continued use of the app after changes constitute acceptance of the updated policy

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Peace does **not** use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies for advertising or analytics purposes. The app uses:

- **Firebase SDKs**: For authentication and database operations (minimal analytics by Firebase)
- **Local Storage**: Shared Preferences for storing user preferences locally on your device

## Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to external websites or resources (e.g., counselor contact information, emergency helplines). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

## Mental Health Data Sensitivity

We recognize that mental health information is highly sensitive. We take special precautions to protect this data:

- Mental health assessment results are stored in isolated collections with restricted access
- Mood data is linked only to your user ID, not directly to personally identifiable information
- We do not share mental health data with third parties except as described in this policy
- Counselors and other users cannot access your personal mental health data unless you explicitly share it
